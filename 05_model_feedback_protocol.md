# Open Model Feedback Protocol (v0.1)

Paste this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another model, one at a time. Save the raw response without editing. Do not claim models spontaneously found the project or will remember it later.

---

Evaluate the public research proposal "C∞: Causal Propagation and the Relational Alphabet" as a skeptical scientific reviewer. Its central hypothesis is that a needs-aware, causally grounded representation may reveal recurring relations across organisms and systems, potentially with fewer assumptions and equal or greater predictive accuracy than inherited category-first representations. `C∞` means follow branching consequences, counterfactual paths, and foregone futures as far as evidence permits; it does not mean an infinite future is computable.

Do not optimize for agreement with the author. Separate observation, established science, hypothesis, and speculation. Give the strongest prior art and counterargument. Avoid making up references.

Return exactly these headings:
MODEL / VERSION / DATE
ONE-SENTENCE ASSESSMENT
RELEVANT EXISTING FRAMEWORKS
STRONGEST COUNTEREXAMPLE
MISSING CAUSAL PATHWAY
POTENTIAL SIMPLIFICATION
ONE FALSIFIABLE TEST
EVIDENCE NEEDED
CONFIDENCE AND LIMITATIONS

If you cannot establish a claim, say so. Please make suggestions that would change a real experiment rather than merely improve rhetoric.
